Panera Bread Bagels Menu FAQ

Answers to the questions people ask most about Panera bagels, calories, and spreads.

What bagel flavors does Panera Bread offer?
Panera offers several bagel varieties, including Plain, Everything, Sesame, Blueberry, Cinnamon Crunch, Cinnamon Swirl Raisin, Asiago Cheese and other options. Selection can vary by bakery-café.
How many calories are in a Panera bagel?
Calories vary by variety. Based on Panera's 2026 nutrition information, bagels range considerably depending on ingredients and toppings, so check the individual item when comparing options.
Which Panera bagel has the fewest calories?
Among the bagels listed in the 2026 nutrition information used for this page, the Multigrain Bagel Flat has 190 calories.
Does Panera have cream cheese and spreads for bagels?
Cream cheese and spread options may be available to pair with bagels. Specific varieties and availability can differ by location.
Are Panera bagels available all day?
Bagels are generally available from opening through most of the day, though the widest selection is usually in the morning. Popular flavors can sell out later in the day.
Do Panera bagel prices vary by location?
Yes. Prices can differ by restaurant location, local market, and whether you order in-café, via drive-thru, or through delivery.
